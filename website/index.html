Pit stop

⌘Q closes the window, not your agents. Their terminals keep running and come back with the scrollback on the next launch.

Native

A Rust app drawn on the GPU with GPUI, Zed's UI framework. No web view: terminals are parsed and drawn in the same process, so a wall of busy agents stays light. More

Also

Sessions on agw VMs show up next to local agents, and a pitwall CLI lets you or an agent add more; anything that touches another machine waits for your OK in the app.

Race Engineer

Optional: an assistant agent for Pitwall itself, on the agent and subscription you already use. Ask it to set up agw, arrange spaces or tune settings; it works through the pitwall CLI, and risky steps still wait for your OK. Nothing runs until you open it.

Coming

A background service with a menu-bar item that keeps your agents running while the app is closed. Not in a build yet. Roadmap
